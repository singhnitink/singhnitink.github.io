About Me

Current Position

Since November 2024, I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Michigan State University, supervised by Dr. Josh Vermaas. My work integrates high-performance molecular simulations, computational chemistry/biology, and machine learning to address critical challenges in drug discovery, membrane transport dynamics, and biofuel production.

Ph.D. and Research Background (July 2019 – November 2024)

I completed my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from IIT Gandhinagar (July 2019 – November 2024) under Dr. Mithun Radhakrishna. My dissertation and research established strong expertise in computational chemistry, advanced molecular simulations, and data analysis.

Key areas of my doctoral work include:

  • Helical Stability: Probing the structural dynamics and stability of charged peptides.
  • Protein Orientation: Investigating and tuning electrostatic interactions to control the adsorption orientation of proteins (e.g., GFP) on charged surfaces.
  • Scientific Software: Designing and implementing statistical models and predictive algorithms for protein sequence/structure analysis.
Tools Developed

During my Ph.D., I developed several computational tools for protein analysis:

  • PSSA: A web-based platform for analyzing protein sequences and structures.
  • HydroDisPred: Predicts intrinsically disordered regions based on hydrophobicity.
  • LigPlot3D: Interactive 3D visualization of protein-ligand interactions from PDB structures.
Research Interests

I leverage high-performance molecular dynamics simulations and machine learning architectures to solve complex problems at the intersection of chemistry, biology, and materials science.

My primary research vectors include:

  • Membrane Transport & Biophysics: Quantifying small-molecule permeation barriers and lipid-protein interactions to optimize biofuel tolerance and drug transport pathways.
  • AI-Driven Protein Design: Deploying generative diffusion models (RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN) and structural transformers (AlphaFold3, ESMFold) to engineer de novo protein binders.
  • Thermodynamics & Enhanced Sampling: Applying metadynamics, FEP, and umbrella sampling to map free energy profiles of peptide folding and protein adsorption orientation on charged interfaces.

1. Computational Biophysics & Membrane Transport Dynamics

My current postdoctoral research focuses on characterizing how small molecules, inhibitors, and proteins interact with complex cell membranes. By simulating these environments at high resolution, I quantify transport dynamics that directly impact industrial biotechnology and health sciences.

  • Inhibitor Permeation: Investigating the structural mechanisms and free-energy barriers governing lignocellulosic inhibitor transport in biofuel-producing bacteria (Zymomonas mobilis).
  • Alcohol Tolerance: Modeling the nanoscale effects of cyclopropane fatty acids and hopanoids in bacterial membranes to enhance tolerance during industrial fermentation processes.
  • High-Performance Tools: Leveraging large-scale systems running in parallel environments using GROMACS, AMBER, NAMD, and OpenMM.

2. AI-Driven Macromolecular Design & Structural Informatics

I specialize in integrating modern deep learning algorithms into the structural biology workflow to design, validate, and predict macromolecular targets.

  • De Novo Binder Design: Utilizing diffusion architectures (RFdiffusion) and inverse folding models (ProteinMPNN) to design mini-protein binders targeting therapeutic checkpoints like PD-L1.
  • Automated Structural Docking: Building end-to-end Python pipelines to bridge AI structures (AlphaFold3) with classical docking packages (AutoDock Vina) to automate high-throughput drug screening workflows.
  • Sequence Disorderness Modeling: Developing custom predictive algorithms (e.g., HydroDisPred) utilizing sliding-window calculations of charge-hydrophobicity boundaries.

3. Peptide Thermodynamics & Charge-Driven Surface Interactions

Drawing from my doctoral training, I explore how charges, sequences, and environmental parameters regulate peptide folded states and their binding states on material interfaces.

  • Enhanced Sampling Methods: Applying metadynamics, replica exchange (REMD/REUS), and umbrella sampling to solve free energy profiles of peptide transitions.
  • Adsorption Control: Tuning electrostatic interactions to direct protein orientation (e.g., Green Fluorescent Protein) during adsorption onto silica and biomaterial surfaces.
  • Self-Assembly & Amyloids: Quantifying the role of sequence patterns and hydrophobic patches in peptide aggregation and self-assembly.

Computational & ML Projects

De Novo Mini Binder Design (RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN)

View Repository

An end-to-end deep learning pipeline to generate de novo mini-protein binders against human PD-L1. By integrating generative diffusion models, inverse folding networks, and alignment-free folding prediction, the workflow designs high-specificity protein candidates targeting known structural hotspots.

Deep Learning Frameworks Used:

  • RFdiffusion: Generative diffusion modeling for structural backbone generation
  • ProteinMPNN: Neural network-based inverse folding for sequence design
  • ESMFold: Large language model (LLM) for alignment-free structure validation

Key Assessment Metrics:

  • pLDDT > 80: Confidence filter for structural fold stability
  • Cα RMSD < 2.0 Å: Fit metric comparing predicted structures against backbones
  • Exploratory MD: Rotamer and contact persistence in OpenMM

AlphaFold3 Structural Docking Pipeline (AutoDock Vina)

View Repository

A robust, automated pipeline to compute thermodynamic binding affinities of protein-ligand complexes predicted by AlphaFold3. The system parses AlphaFold3-formatted JSON structures, reconstructs coordinate files, parameterizes receptor/ligand targets, automatically sets optimal search spaces, and schedules parallel docking simulations.

Automated Preparation:

  • OpenBabel Integration: Reconstructs 3D coordinates from SMILES codes
  • Polar Hydrogen Addition: Sets correct protonation states for receptor & ligand
  • Autogrid Alignment: Dynamically bounds the search grid box around the active site

Calculations & Workflows:

  • AutoDock Vina Engine: Computes binding energy outputs in kcal/mol
  • Pose Sorting: Ranks candidate conformations by affinity scores
  • Reproducible Environment: Self-contained setup shell script for cloud architectures

HydroDisPred (Predictive Model for Protein Disorder)

View Repository

A Python-based predictive modeling tool to identify Intrinsically Disordered Regions (IDRs) in proteins. It evaluates physical parameters derived from sequence metrics (hydrophobic density vs net charge) via an optimized sliding-window algorithm, outputting sequence disorderness mappings.

Algorithm & Parameters:

  • Feature Selection: Local charge composition & nonpolar abundance
  • Hyperparameters: Custom sliding binsize & detection cutoff threshold
  • Binary Classifications: Dynamic sequence-state assignment

Applications:

  • IDP Screening: High-throughput screening of uncharacterized proteins
  • Visual Mapping: Auto-generates residue-level disorder profiles
  • Validation: Backed by published empirical sequence properties
